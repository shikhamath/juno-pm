## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Executive dashboard
- **Value proposition:** Take the info from slack channels and Jiras and summarize the info into multiple categories. Also list top 5 priorities

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | Minimum 5 issues in a single category for it to be valid | Yes / No | less that 5 issues (makes the category invalid) | n/a | Issue 1 -  5 or more with citations |
| Hallucination check (deal-breaker) | Does each cateogry show actual issues with citations | Yes / No | The usability of the feature is not good (Source: none found) | n/a | The usability of the feature is not good' (Source: Jira number xxxxxx) |
| Usability | Is the table and categories understandable to an executive | 1 to 5 | Not easy to understand the issues, row & columns ar enot easily understand | High level understanding of the categories  | Ordered rows and columns with clear categories of issues. Priorities and next steps are clearly listed |
| Clarity of verbiage | Will an exec understand the description & priorities | Yes / No | Technical jargon, incomplete sentences, end user pain point not explained | n/a | The issue is explained including end user pain point |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 2 answers a day
- **Who scores them with you:** Me plus one more PM and engineering lead
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** If we disagree, we discuss the scoring and clarify. Iterate on our criteria

## Pass bar

All Yes answers and rating of 5 where relevant

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Hallucination check: any "no"
- A single hallucination
